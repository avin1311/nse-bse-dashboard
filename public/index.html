Risk & position size

New calls get a quantity (stocks) or number of lots (index options) so that a stop-loss hit costs about your risk per trade, capped by your capital (intraday: capital × leverage). Calls show ₹ at risk and ₹ profit / loss. When today's Trade Calls reach the daily loss stop (closed + open), no new calls are made for the rest of the day; open ones keep running. Paper trading: nothing is ordered.

Positions

Paper positions only — nothing here places a real order. While this tab is open, targets/stop-losses are checked every few seconds and trigger the banner at the top of the page. They're also checked server-side on a schedule (independent of your browser) and sent to Telegram if configured — see the README for setup. Each position also gets an automatic one-time deviation warning (amber ⚠️) at the halfway point between entry and stop-loss, as an early heads-up before the hard stop actually hits.

Backtest a strategy

Runs against real daily closing prices via the local proxy when it's reachable (up to 2 years), otherwise a synthetic extended series. See the chat for a summary of what published backtests report for each strategy, and read the caveat about overfitting before trusting any win rate — including this tool's.

Results are net of costs: brokerage, STT, exchange fees, stamp duty and GST ≈ 0.12% per side for delivery trades, plus slippage. Set both to 0 for gross results.

Options & futures scanning

Real Upstox option-chain data once UPSTOX_ACCESS_TOKEN is configured. The "signal" here is the standard OI-buildup classification traders use (Long/Short Buildup, Long Unwinding, Short Covering) — derived from price direction + open-interest direction together, since RSI/MACD-style indicators don't translate meaningfully to option premiums. The very first scan of any stock/expiry shows "No comparison yet" since there's nothing prior to compare against — scan again a few minutes later to see real classifications.
